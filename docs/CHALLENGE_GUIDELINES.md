# 🐍 Python Challenge Guidelines

These guidelines establish how members should approach, complete, discuss, and learn from Python challenges.

## 1. 🎯 The Objective

The purpose of each challenge is to develop independent problem-solving, Python proficiency, logical reasoning, and the ability to explain code.

Getting the correct output is important, but understanding why your solution works is equally important.

## 2. 🧠 The First 30 Minutes

During the first 30 minutes of attempting a challenge, members may use:

- Their own notes.
- Official Python documentation.
- A debugger.
- `print()` statements for inspecting program behaviour.

During this initial period, members should not use AI tools or search for ready-made solutions.

## 3. 🔎 When You Get Stuck

After the initial 30 minutes, members may consult AI tools or other learning resources.

However, members must:

- Understand any information they use.
- Write and test their own implementation.
- Be able to explain their solution.
- Clearly disclose where and how AI assisted them when required by the challenge.

Copying code without understanding it defeats the purpose of the challenge.

## 4. 📤 Completion Confirmation

Once you have completed a challenge, submit the agreed completion confirmation:

**✅ Solved**

Follow any additional submission instructions provided for that particular challenge.

## 5. 🔒 Solution Confidentiality

Before the challenge deadline and group discussion:

- Do not post your solution in the group.
- Do not share code that reveals the solution to other members.
- Do not publish your solution in a publicly accessible repository.

The goal is to give everyone a fair opportunity to attempt the problem independently.

## 6. 💬 Post-Challenge Discussion

After the deadline, members should be prepared to discuss:

- Their approach to the problem.
- The reasoning behind their implementation.
- Difficulties encountered.
- Errors and how they were resolved.
- Alternative approaches and their trade-offs.

Screenshots, code explanations, and reflections may be requested for individual challenges.

## 7. 🪞 Reflection

After completing a challenge, reflect honestly on:

- What you learned.
- What you found difficult.
- Whether you needed external assistance.
- What you would improve in another attempt.

Reflection is part of the learning process, not an optional measure of intelligence.

## 8. 🤝 Respect and Integrity

Members should respect different learning speeds, ask constructive questions, and support one another without revealing solutions prematurely.

Mistakes are expected. Dishonesty about attempts or assistance is discouraged.

## 9. 📈 Continuous Progress

Challenges will gradually introduce new concepts and build on previously learned material. Members should revisit weak areas rather than merely rushing to the next challenge.

**The standard: Attempt independently. Understand deeply. Explain clearly. Improve continuously.**
