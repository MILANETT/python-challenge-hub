# 🤖 AI Usage Policy

## 1. Purpose

The Python Challenge Hub encourages independent thinking, experimentation, and genuine understanding.

AI tools can support learning, but they must not replace the effort required to solve problems and develop programming skills.

The objective is not to avoid AI forever. It is to become capable of using it critically, responsibly, and effectively.

## 2. The First 30 Minutes

During the first 30 minutes of an active challenge attempt, members must not use AI tools to obtain challenge-specific solutions, hints, explanations, debugging assistance, or other help with solving the problem.

During this period, members may use only:

- Their own notes.
- Official Python documentation.
- A debugger.
- `print()` statements for inspecting program behaviour.

This restriction gives members an opportunity to reason independently before seeking external assistance.

## 3. Responsible AI Use After 30 Minutes

After the initial 30 minutes, members may consult AI tools when necessary, subject to any additional challenge-specific instructions.

Appropriate uses include:

- Clarifying a Python concept relevant to the challenge.
- Understanding an error message.
- Learning how a language feature works.
- Reviewing an approach after making an independent attempt.
- Comparing alternative approaches and understanding their trade-offs.
- Identifying weaknesses in code that the member can explain.

AI assistance should help members understand a problem and improve their own implementation, rather than simply generate a submission for them.

## 4. Member Responsibilities

When using AI assistance, members must:

- Understand suggestions before applying them.
- Evaluate whether the suggestions are correct and appropriate.
- Test their implementation, including relevant edge cases.
- Adapt and write code they can explain rather than blindly copying generated solutions.
- Disclose AI assistance wherever the challenge instructions require it.
- Take responsibility for the correctness, quality, and behaviour of their final code.

AI-generated output is not automatically correct. Members should verify claims, code, and explanations rather than trusting them without examination.

## 5. Disclosing AI Assistance

When a challenge requires disclosure, members must clearly document how AI contributed to their work.

For challenges that require reflection in code comments, include a comment describing:

- **What AI helped with:** the concept, error, explanation, or approach.
- **How it helped:** what information or guidance was useful.
- **What you did yourself:** how you evaluated, adapted, implemented, or tested the result.

For example, a member might write:

```python
# AI assistance:
# I asked AI to explain an error message.
# I used the explanation to investigate my code,
# then corrected and tested the implementation myself.
```

This is an illustration of the disclosure format, not a required statement to copy. Each member should describe their actual experience honestly.

Follow the specific challenge instructions if they require a different disclosure format.

## 6. What to Avoid

Members must not:

- Use AI during the restricted first 30 minutes.
- Submit code they cannot explain.
- Present AI-assisted work as entirely independent when disclosure is required.
- Use AI to bypass the intended learning objectives.
- Share solutions or solution-revealing AI output with other members before the deadline and designated discussion.
- Assume AI-generated code is correct without testing it.

## 7. AI After the Challenge

After the deadline and designated group discussion, AI may also be used to deepen understanding, explore alternative implementations, review mistakes, and identify areas for further practice.

Members should still make a genuine effort to understand the material rather than treating a reference solution as something to memorize.

## 8. Learning Over Shortcuts

Needing assistance does not mean you have failed. What matters is whether that assistance leaves you better equipped to solve the next problem independently.

**Use AI to strengthen your understanding, not to substitute for it.**
