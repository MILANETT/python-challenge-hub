# 🤖 AI Usage Policy

## 1. Purpose

The Python Challenge Hub encourages independent thinking, experimentation, and genuine understanding.

AI tools can support learning, but they should not replace the effort required to solve problems and develop programming skills.

## 2. The First 30 Minutes

During the first 30 minutes of a challenge attempt, members must not use AI tools to obtain solutions, hints, or explanations for the challenge.

Members may use:

- Their own notes.
- Official Python documentation.
- A debugger.
- `print()` statements.

The goal is to practise reasoning independently before seeking external assistance.

## 3. Responsible AI Use After 30 Minutes

After the initial period, members may consult AI tools when necessary.

Appropriate uses include:

- Clarifying a Python concept.
- Understanding an error message.
- Learning how a language feature works.
- Reviewing an approach after making an independent attempt.
- Understanding alternative implementations after the challenge discussion.

AI should support the learning process rather than do the thinking on the member's behalf.

## 4. Member Responsibilities

When using AI assistance, members must:

- Understand the suggested ideas before applying them.
- Test their own code.
- Rewrite and adapt code themselves rather than blindly copying it.
- Be able to explain the final implementation.
- Disclose AI assistance wherever the challenge instructions require it.

Members remain responsible for the correctness and quality of their submissions.

## 5. What to Avoid

Members should not:

- Use AI during the restricted first 30 minutes.
- Submit code they cannot explain.
- Present AI-generated work as entirely independent when disclosure is required.
- Use AI to bypass the purpose of a challenge.
- Share AI-generated solutions with other members before the deadline.

## 6. Learning Over Shortcuts

Needing assistance does not mean you have failed. The important thing is to use assistance in a way that leaves you more capable of solving the next problem independently.

**Use AI to strengthen your understanding, not to substitute for it.**
